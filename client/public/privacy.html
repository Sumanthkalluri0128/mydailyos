Privacy Policy

FlexFit (“we”, “us”) · Last updated: 4 October 2026

FlexFit is a personal health and daily-planning app (website at mydailyos.vercel.app and a mobile app). This page explains what information we collect, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. In short: your data is yours, we use it only to run the app for you, and we do not sell it or show ads.

1. Information we collect

Account information. Your name and email address, and a password (stored only as a one-way hash, never in readable form). If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google tells us your name, email address and a Google account ID so we can sign you in. We do not receive your Google password and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, Contacts or any other Google data.

Information you enter. Profile details (such as age, sex, height, weight, goal weight, activity level and targets) and your logs: food and meals, water, weight, exercise, steps, health measurements, fasting, tasks and habits. Some of this is health-related; you decide what to enter.

From your phone (mobile app only, with your permission).

  • Physical activity / step count from your phone’s step counter, to show steps and estimate calories burned.
  • Camera, to scan food barcodes and to take progress photos.
  • Photos, if you pick a progress photo from your library.
  • Notifications, for reminders you turn on. Reminders are scheduled on your phone.

Progress photos stay on your device. They are saved only on your phone and are never uploaded to our servers.

Technical information. Our servers and hosting providers may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and reliability. If error reporting is enabled, anonymous crash and error details may be sent to an error-monitoring service.

2. How we use your information

  • To create your account, sign you in, and keep you signed in.
  • To store and show your logs, charts, streaks, goals and calorie/water/step targets.
  • To email you a one-time code when you ask to reset your password. These are the only emails we send.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix bugs.

We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with data brokers.

3. Who handles your data

We use trusted service providers to run FlexFit. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Hosting: Vercel (website) and Render (server).
  • Database: a hosted MongoDB database where your account and logs are stored.
  • Google: for “Continue with Google” sign-in, and to deliver password-reset emails through our Gmail account.
  • Open Food Facts: when you scan a barcode, the barcode number (and nothing about you) is sent to look up the product.
  • Error monitoring (if enabled): to detect crashes.

We may also disclose information if required by law. FlexFit’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy.

4. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed, password-reset codes are stored hashed and expire after 30 minutes, and sign-in attempts are rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Access & export: in Profile you can download your data as files.
  • Edit: you can change or delete any entry, and update your profile and password at any time.
  • Delete your account: in Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all your logs from our database. It cannot be undone.
  • Permissions: you can turn off camera, photo, activity and notification access in your phone’s settings at any time.

If you cannot use the in-app options, contact us (see below) and we will help.

6. Data retention

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is removed from our database. Hosting providers may keep short-lived backups or logs for a limited time before they are overwritten.

7. Children

FlexFit is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Not medical advice

FlexFit provides general wellness estimates (such as calorie and water targets). It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Talk to a qualified professional before making health decisions.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app.
